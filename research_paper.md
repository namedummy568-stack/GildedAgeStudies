### Aftermath of Wounded Knee

The Wounded Knee Massacre in December 1890 marked a tragic end to the American Indian Wars. The aftermath saw increased efforts by the U.S. government to suppress Native American religious and cultural practices, further accelerating assimilation policies. The event also galvanized Native American resistance movements in the long term, leading to renewed calls for self-determination and the preservation of indigenous cultures. The memory of Wounded Knee continues to serve as a powerful symbol of injustice and resilience.
